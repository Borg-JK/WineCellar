Bottle photos in this folder come from Open Food Facts (https://world.openfoodfacts.org),
contributed by its users and licensed CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
They were resized for this app. Each file is named after the product barcode;
the source page is https://world.openfoodfacts.org/product/<barcode>.
